Which assistant personality best fits which client. THE CORE IDEA

Drivers pair with Executors

Every archetype rolls up to one of eight trait families, each with its own gemstone. Four are drivers (they set direction); four are executors (they deliver, stabilize, and care for people). In a client→assistant hierarchy, a driver client pairs best with an executor assistant — and vice-versa. Two drivers contest the wheel; two of the same family amplify a shared blind spot.

LAYER 2

OCEAN trait refinement

Once the family pairing is set, tune with the Big Five. Two traits are complementary (the assistant covers), two match (align styles), one adapts. Drawn from the "ideal subordinate" guidance on the 243 OCEAN outcomes.

TraitRuleWhyTarget
CConscientiousnessCOMPLEMENT — assistant HighThe biggest lever. Whatever the client scores, the assistant should be High-C — the "structural integrator" who ships reliable work. Largest lift when the client is Low-C.assistant C ≥ client C; aim High
NNeuroticismCOMPLEMENT — assistant LowStability is a universal asset. A Low-N assistant absorbs pressure. Critical when the client is High-N — they need a calm anchor, never a second worrier.assistant N ≤ client N; aim Low
EExtraversionMATCH — sync the energyHigh-E clients want a vocal brainstorming partner; Low-E clients want quiet, asynchronous check-ins. A mismatch reads as social friction.assistant E ≈ client E
AAgreeablenessMATCH — sync conflict styleHigh-A clients want an empathetic collaborator; Low-A clients want a direct operator who defends ideas in debate. Match so their values align.assistant A ≈ client A
OOpennessADAPT — within one bandKeep the assistant within ±1 band. A High-O client who loves pivots needs an adaptable (Med–High O) and High-C assistant so novelty still ships.assistant O within ±1 of client O

  1. Family (Layer 1): Catalyst client → Stabilizer is Ideal (the "structural integrator"); Analyzer is Strong. Avoid another Catalyst.
  2. C — complement: client is Low-C, so the assistant must be High-C — the biggest single fit driver.
  3. N — complement: client is High-N, so the assistant should be Low-N to anchor them.
  4. E & A — match: client is High-E / Low-A → assistant High-E, Low-A.
  5. O — adapt: client is High-O → assistant Med–High-O to keep pace without adding chaos.
